# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that
contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy and kindness, being respectful of differing opinions
and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the
community. Examples of unacceptable behavior include sexualized language or
imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, harassment in public or private, publishing others' private
information without explicit permission, and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

One project-specific norm: audits name datasets, not people. Findings about
public benchmarks are welcome here; speculation about the intent or
competence of their authors is not. Every flawed dataset this project has
audited was built by people advancing their field, and the tone of any
report, issue, or discussion should reflect that.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer at erwinrandy3@gmail.com. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer is
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter. Maintainers
who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may be temporarily or
permanently removed from the project.
