# Paper

The SynthAudit methodology paper is under submission to the Journal of
Machine Learning Research and is intentionally not committed here while
under review. This folder will receive the arXiv preprint link, the
camera-ready PDF, and supplementary material on acceptance.

Until then: the software citation lives in the repository root
(`CITATION.cff`), the audit reports backing the paper's case studies are in
`reports/`, the ablation and stability tables in `benchmarks/`, and the
publication figures in `figures/`.
